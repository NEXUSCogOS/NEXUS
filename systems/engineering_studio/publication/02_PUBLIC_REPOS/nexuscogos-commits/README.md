# NexusCogOS

Independent AI systems and research engineering focused on:

- agentic engineering systems
- AI evaluation
- artificial memory
- evidence and provenance
- reproducible experimentation
- governed autonomous engineering

## Research Programme

### Agentic Engineering Benchmark

Reproducible evaluation of software-engineering agent architectures.

### Engineering Studio

Research into evidence-grounded autonomous engineering systems.

### AI Evidence & Provenance

Methods for connecting AI-generated engineering claims to evidence,
experiments and provenance.

### Cumulative Agent Memory

Experimental investigation of persistent and structured memory for
long-horizon AI agents.

## Research Principles

Results should be measurable, reproducible and appropriately qualified.

Negative results and limitations are treated as part of the scientific
record.

## Status

Public research programme under development.
