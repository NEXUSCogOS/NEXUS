# NEXUS-EVA Separation of Duties

## Engineering Studio

MAY:
- prepare protocols;
- construct verification packages;
- prepare internal evidence;
- create evaluation harnesses;
- identify candidate verifiers.

MAY NOT:
- impersonate an external verifier;
- independently validate its own work;
- manufacture external attestations.

## External Verifier

MAY:
- challenge methodology;
- reject inadequate methodology;
- substitute stronger evaluation methods;
- reproduce experiments;
- conduct adversarial evaluation;
- identify deficiencies;
- return positive or negative results.

## NEXUS-EVA

MAY:
- register claims;
- register verifiers;
- freeze packages;
- record dispatch;
- receive external evidence;
- calculate hashes;
- verify integrity;
- record independence/conflicts;
- bind evidence to claims;
- adjudicate scope;
- preserve original verdicts;
- seal evidence.

MAY NOT:
- execute experiments;
- manufacture external verdicts;
- mutate external verdicts;
- simulate independent human review;
- transition Experimental Validation state;
- mutate the Experimental Validation registry.

## Experimental Validation

MAY consume properly governed EVA evidence according to its own authority
model.

NEXUS-EVA supplies evidence references.

NEXUS-EVA does not grant experiment-transition authority.
