# NEXUS-EVA Hard Invariants

EVA-INV-001 — NO SELF-VERIFICATION
A system or investigator materially responsible for a result cannot count
its own assessment as independent verification.

EVA-INV-002 — NO VERDICT MANUFACTURE
NEXUS-EVA cannot manufacture an artifact represented as an external verdict.

EVA-INV-003 — ORIGINAL EVIDENCE PRESERVATION
Original externally returned evidence must be preserved byte-for-byte.

EVA-INV-004 — NO VERDICT MUTATION
External PASS, PARTIAL, FAIL or INCONCLUSIVE verdicts cannot be rewritten.

EVA-INV-005 — CLAIM SPECIFICITY
Verification applies only to claims actually evaluated.

EVA-INV-006 — NO AUTOMATIC PASS
Installation, hashing, package creation, dispatch, receipt or schema
validation cannot imply scientific validity.

EVA-INV-007 — INDEPENDENCE IS EVIDENTIAL
Reviewer independence requires identity, role, prior-involvement and
conflict information.

EVA-INV-008 — UNKNOWN FAILS CLOSED
UNKNOWN independence cannot satisfy an independent-verification requirement.

EVA-INV-009 — INTEGRITY BEFORE ADJUDICATION
Returned evidence must satisfy required integrity controls before
adjudication.

EVA-INV-010 — NEGATIVE EVIDENCE PRESERVED
FAIL, PARTIAL and INCONCLUSIVE findings receive equivalent provenance
protection.

EVA-INV-011 — NO EXPERIMENT EXECUTION
NEXUS-EVA has no experiment-execution authority.

EVA-INV-012 — NO EXPERIMENT STATE TRANSITION
NEXUS-EVA cannot transition Experimental Validation between BLOCKED, READY,
FROZEN, EXECUTED or other experiment states.

EVA-INV-013 — NO SIMULATED HUMAN ATTESTATION
Machine-generated evidence cannot silently satisfy a requirement for
independent human attestation.

EVA-INV-014 — PACKAGE FREEZE
Mutation after PACKAGE_FROZEN requires a new version and cryptographic
identity.

EVA-INV-015 — PROTOCOL FREEZE
Frozen hypotheses/endpoints cannot be silently changed after outcome
observation.

EVA-INV-016 — EXACT EVIDENCE BINDING
Verification must bind to the exact claim, protocol, package and system
version evaluated.

EVA-INV-017 — CONFLICT DISCLOSURE
Material conflicts of interest must be recorded and preserved.

EVA-INV-018 — SCOPE DISCIPLINE
Findings cannot be generalized beyond their evaluated scope.

EVA-INV-019 — STANDARDS DISCIPLINE
A standards crosswalk does not constitute certification or accreditation.

EVA-INV-020 — FAIL CLOSED
Missing mandatory identity, provenance, integrity or independence evidence
prevents progression to dependent verification states.
